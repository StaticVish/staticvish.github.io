Hello World: From GW BASIC to Cloud Architecture

May 1, 2024 •1 min read

Table of Contents

I wrote my first lines of code in GW BASIC growing up in a small town in Southern India.

Back then, computers were rare, highly expensive machines. We had to take our shoes off just to cross the threshold of the school computer lab. A single continuous beep from one of the 80286 XT-PCs would send the lab attendants sprinting over, terrified that you had somehow broken the hardware.

And then there were the 5¼-inch floppy disks. Because there was no Master Boot Record (MBR) on a hard drive, you had to physically insert a DOS boot floppy just to start the machine—exactly like using a crank rod on the bonnet of a vintage car to get the engine turning.

The good old days of the 80286 and the big red toggle switch.

This blog is a personal sandbox to document my evolution as an engineer—from those early days of boot floppies and GW BASIC to modern cloud architecture, DevOps, and infrastructure engineering.

It’s not meant for the absolute “Gurus” who already know it all. It is just a place to share what I’ve learned, document the systems I build, and occasionally drop a Tambrahm recipe or a motorcycle route along the way. No fluff, just the things I enjoy building and doing.
