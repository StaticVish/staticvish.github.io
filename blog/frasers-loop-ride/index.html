The Bentong - Fraser's Loop: A Public Holiday Ride

May 25, 2026 •2 min read

Table of Contents

  • 🗺️ The Route Map
  • ⏱️ The Relaxed Itinerary (~8.5 Hours)
  • ⛽ Fuel & Weather Estimates

Whenever a public holiday rolls around, getting out early before the heat and traffic is essential. This route is an unbroken, ~220km loop starting from Puchong, offering high-speed straights in the morning and intense twisties up to Fraser’s Hill mid-day.

The best part? Zero overlapping roads and a relaxed pace suitable for middle-aged riders who want to enjoy the corners without burning out.

🗺️ The Route Map

Google Maps: PETRONAS Puchong Gateway ➔ Bentong ➔ Fraser’s Hill ➔ KKB ➔ Ulu Yam ➔ Rajabhojana, Puchong

⏱️ The Relaxed Itinerary (~8.5 Hours)

  • 07:00 AM – Kickstand Up
    • Location: Petronas Puchong Gateway. Fuel up to full!
    • Pace: Highway straights & sweepers to Bentong (via LDP/MRR2 to Karak).
  • 08:15 AM – Breakfast & Morning Coffee (1 Hour)
    • Location: Bentong Town.
    • Notes: Take your time with some Idli/Dosa and let the morning traffic clear out. (100% pure veg, no garlic!).
  • 09:15 AM – Attack the Twisties
    • Route: Leave Bentong via Route 8 (Tras) & Route 55.
    • Pace: Take it easy and enjoy the technical corners up the Gap.
  • 10:15 AM – The Summit R&R (1 Hour)
    • Location: Fraser’s Hill Clock Tower.
    • Notes: You’ve conquered the climb! Stretch the legs, hydrate, take photos, and let the bikes cool down properly.
  • 11:15 AM – The Descent
    • Route: Heading down towards Kuala Kubu Bharu (KKB).
    • Pace: Downhill switchbacks. Gentle on the brakes.
  • 12:15 PM – Hydration & Stretch Break (30 Mins)
    • Location: KKB Town / Selangor River Dam area.
    • Notes: Dedicated R&R stop to rest the wrists before the final leg.
  • 12:45 PM – The Ulu Yam Flow
    • Route: KKB ➔ Ulu Yam ➔ Highway back to Puchong.
    • Pace: Medium-speed flowy sweepers transitioning back to city straights.
  • 02:00 PM – Mission Accomplished: Late Lunch (90 Mins)
    • Location: Rajabhojana Indian Cuisine, Puchong.
    • Notes: Park the bikes. 90 minutes to kick back, laugh about the corners, and enjoy a massive, Tambrahm-compliant pure veg feast to cap off the ride!

⛽ Fuel & Weather Estimates

  • Total Distance: ~220 km
  • Estimated Consumption: ~11 liters
  • Cost: ~RM 38.00 (based on unsubsidized market float RON95 at ~RM 3.47/liter)
  • Weather Note: Expect scattered clouds and ~26°C for a crisp morning start. Watch for afternoon showers on the descent through KKB/Ulu Yam.

Ride safe!
